I'm a machine learning researcher in the Medical Imaging and Data Integration Lab at MSU's Institute for Quantitative Health Science and Engineering. Our group works with CDC/NIOSH on screening chest X-rays for pneumoconiosis, the lung disease miners and industrial workers get from breathing coal and silica dust.

My part is adapting vision and multimodal foundation models to this problem, and building the offline desktop application that lets radiologists and B readers actually use them. I also mentor undergraduates in the lab.

I finished my B.S. in Computational Data Science, with a minor in mathematics, at MSU in 2026. Alongside my degree I spent three years at iCER, MSU's HPC center, writing tools for people who run jobs on the cluster. On the side I'm co-founding DockPilot, which makes scheduling software for warehouse loading docks.

Selected work

Chest X-ray screening software for B readers 2026

A desktop application that runs our models entirely offline, so patient images never leave the reading workstation. I lead development: the PySide6 interface, ONNX inference, about 1,860 automated tests, Windows CI, and the installer. It's in beta with radiologists now.

Adapting foundation models to chest radiographs 2026

Fine-tuning and benchmarking vision and multimodal models on our NIOSH data. Held-out accuracy went from 75% to 85% (ROC-AUC 0.80 to 0.96), and LoRA on MedGemma 4B went from 82% to 90%. I'm now working on uncertainty-aware labels for films where expert readers disagree.

Job-script checker for MSU's HPC cluster 2024–2026

Catches mistakes in SLURM scripts before they're submitted, using rule-based checks, ShellCheck, and a local LLM for the fuzzier cases. Deployed on Open OnDemand for MSU researchers. I also built a model that suggests CPU, memory, GPU, and wall-time requests from past job data.

poster

Legal document search with cited answers 2026

A local retrieval system over PDFs and Word files. It answers questions, quotes the passage each claim comes from, and declines when it can't find support. Keyword search in PostgreSQL plus vector search in Qdrant, with a local model through Ollama.
